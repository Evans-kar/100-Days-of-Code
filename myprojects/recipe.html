How to make pancakes

In this webpage I will teach you how to make sweet pancakes with only eggs, milk, baking flour and vanilla essence.

After this comeback and let me know if they are the sweets pancakes you ever had. Just make sure to follow this simple recipe from start to finish

ingredients

  • 2 cups of all purpose wheat flour
  • sugar
  • salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 drop essence
  • Water
  • Cooking Oil

Instructions

  1. First, you add the wheat flour, 1 tablespoon of baking powder, enough sugar to taste, a pinch of salt and stir the mixture.
  2. Add water to the mixture until it's intact, Note:don't put so much water just little by little as you stir.
  3. Once the mixture is intact and well stirred and fine.. Add the two beaten eggs that had been stirred in a different bowl into the mixture. And continue to stir until all the lumps of the flour cannot be seen.
  4. Serve with tea or cold milk
